Pilot program — 30 days free.

Try Counterrow in your own shop for 30 days — full product, no card, and we set it up with you. Here is exactly what you get, what you need to do, and what it costs after the trial.

Start your pilot — chat on WhatsApp

What you get

One system for the whole shop — on your phone and on your PC.

One app, everything

Billing, stock, credit (khata) and your shop's own website — in one place.

On your phone

Bill at the counter from the phone app, and stock and credit update themselves.

From your credit register

A photo of your old register builds your credit book — no retyping page by page.

Credit reminders

Who owes what, in one list — reminders ready to send on WhatsApp, so trapped money comes back.

Weekly report

What you earned this week, and how much credit came back — in one short report.

What you need to do

Four things — that is all.

1. An Android phone

A phone with internet, for the shopkeeper app.

2. One person at the counter

A dedicated person who rings sales through Counterrow — this is the most important part.

3. Your registers

Your stock list or a photo of the stock register, plus a photo of your credit (khata) register.

4. One to two hours

Setup and training — and we ring the first sale together.

Pricing

After the trial: Basic Rs 5,499 (~$20) a month or Pro Rs 7,999 (~$28) a month, website hosting included. Yearly: pay for 10 months, use for 12.
