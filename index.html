The problems are money problems

The agent drafts. The owner decides.

Counterrow's agent layer reads the shop's own ledger — stock, sales, khata, profit — and drafts the day's work: a low-stock purchase order, a clearance post for dead stock, a WhatsApp reply, the day-closing summary, a cart reminder. Nothing is sent, posted or paid until the owner approves it in one queue. No decision is ever taken without the owner.

And the shop's pages are written so AI assistants can read and quote them — when a customer asks an AI where to buy, the shop can be the answer. That is the next step of search, built into every Counterrow shop from the start.

A website should not be a second shop to run.

A shopkeeper who gets a website made gets a second job: products added by hand, prices changed twice, every small edit needs a professional — a wait and a bill. And the worst part: the website never knows what is actually in stock, so customers order things the shop no longer has.

Counterrow has one record. Add a product once in the dashboard and it appears on the website. Change a price once and it changes everywhere. And stock is always true — a sale at the counter deducts stock at once, so the website never sells what the shop does not have. No code, no professional, no monthly bill.
